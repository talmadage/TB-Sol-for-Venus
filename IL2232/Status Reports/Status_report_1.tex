\section{Risks / Action plan (-> Paul)}

On this part we will follow-up and update the risk analysis we previously did. It’s important here to state, as much as possible, the risks the sponsor and steering committee need to be aware of, and to propose actions to reduce those ones.

\subsection{Follow-up of Risk Analysis}

The initial risk analysis was performed at the beginning of the project, however it is necessary to re-evaluate the risks we listed and possibly add new ones. That is why we try to follow up on risks and update the risk matrix when new issues appear.\\
The mitigation strategies defined in the initial risk analysis have already proven his use in practice. Indeed, several of the identified risks have already occurred but were mitigated.\\ 
For example:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Toolchain dependency} - we have tested the compiler early and it helped us detect errors quickly to manage them.
    \item \textbf{Hardware issues} - we also tested the hardware as soon as possible and we have seen non-functional cable and power adapter incompatibilities that we succeeded to solve without delaying development.
    \item \textbf{Environment setup} - for some of us with unusual systems or machines, this part wasn’t that easy. And sharing documentation and tips within the group ensured that all members have a working FPGA and synthesis environment.
\end{itemize}
